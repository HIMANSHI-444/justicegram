About JusticeGram

An AI-powered legal literacy, problem categorization, and legal aid discovery platform.

1. Project Background & Motivation

In India and many developing jurisdictions, ordinary citizens and college students encounter everyday legal issues—such as arbitrary withholding of college marksheets, security deposit deductions by landlords, workplace wage non-payment, consumer e-commerce fraud, and UPI cyber scams. However, navigating the legal ecosystem remains daunting due to complex statutory jargon, lack of awareness regarding appropriate dispute resolution forums, and unfamiliarity with free legal aid mechanisms provided under the Legal Services Authorities Act (1987).

JusticeGram was developed as a BCA final/mini-project to bridge this literacy gap. By combining natural language processing (NLP) with curated legal knowledge repositories, the platform enables citizens to describe grievances in natural English or conversational Hinglish, understand the broad domain their issue belongs to, and connect directly with student legal aid clinics, DLSA services, and pro bono legal counsel.

2. Core Technical Architecture

Frontend & UI/UX
  • Responsive HTML5, CSS3 & JavaScript (ES6)
  • Bootstrap 5.3 + Custom Navy (#142133) & Cream (#F4EDE0) Styling
  • Bootstrap Icons (SVG glyphs)
  • Chart.js for interactive analytics visualizations
Backend & Database
  • Python 3 with Flask modular architecture
  • Flask-SQLAlchemy ORM with SQLite database
  • Werkzeug PBKDF2 secure password hashing
  • Role-based access control (Admin & User roles)
Machine Learning & NLP
  • TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency)
  • Multi-class Logistic Regression with class-balancing across 15 categories
  • Rule-based keyword heuristic fallback & subcategory detection
  • Ambiguity & brevity detection safety guards
Ethical Guardrails
  • Strict informational disclaimers on all outputs
  • 26 attributable Indian statutory references with India Code links
  • Official helplines (1930, 1915, 1091, 15100, 1800-180-5522)
  • 16 transparently labeled demonstration advocate profiles

3. College Project Viva & Evaluation Notes

For academic examiners and evaluators:

  • Dataset Attribution: The training dataset consists of synthetic and educational legal problem prompts designed for demonstration purposes, covering all 10 target categories. It can be retrained or expanded via app/ai/train_model.py.
  • Offline Resiliency: The application does not depend on proprietary paid external LLM APIs; the local scikit-learn model and rule engine operate completely offline and deterministically.
  • Database Portability: The database layer utilizes standard SQLAlchemy models and can easily transition to MySQL, MariaDB, or PostgreSQL by changing the DATABASE_URI environment variable.
