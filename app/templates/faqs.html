JusticeGram is a legal technology platform built to assist college students, youth, and ordinary citizens. It helps users understand which broad legal domain their everyday issues belong to (such as education certificate retention, tenancy security deposits, cyber financial scams, or consumer disputes), and points them to appropriate remedies, necessary documents, and free legal aid.

Private lawyers charge a professional consultation fee or case retainer. Free Legal Aid is provided through state institutions (like the District Legal Services Authority - DLSA, Taluk Legal Services Committees, and University Legal Clinics) under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987. Eligible citizens receive free legal counseling, drafting, and government-appointed panel lawyers without paying legal fees.

Under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, the following persons are entitled to free legal aid:
  • Women and children
  • Members of Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST)
  • Industrial workmen and laborers
  • Victims of mass disasters, violence, flood, drought, or earthquake
  • Persons with disabilities
  • Under-trial prisoners in custody
  • Persons whose annual income does not exceed the statutory ceiling (ranging from ₹1,00,000 to ₹3,00,000 depending on state rules)
