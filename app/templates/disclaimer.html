Legal Disclaimer & Terms

Important disclosures regarding AI legal categorization and informational limits.

Not Legal Advice: The information generated by JusticeGram’s AI Legal Assistant, the document checklists, and the category predictions provided on this website are exclusively for educational, legal literacy, and academic research purposes.
1. No Attorney-Client Relationship

Using this website, submitting descriptions of legal issues, or viewing AI-generated next steps does not establish an advocate-client relationship between you and JusticeGram, its developers, or any listed advocate. An advocate-client relationship is only created when you formally retain an advocate through an explicit written agreement.

2. Limitations of Artificial Intelligence & NLP Models

The AI classification module uses machine learning and natural language heuristics to predict broad legal categories. While designed to aid initial discovery, AI classification may be incomplete, imperfect, or unsuited for multifaceted or highly nuanced legal disputes. Users must not rely solely on automated suggestions when making critical legal, financial, or procedural decisions.

3. Verification of Advocates & Legal Aid Listings

Advocates displayed as "Verified" have submitted documentation to platform administrators. Profiles marked as "Demo" or "Unverified" are educational demonstration fixtures. Users are advised to independently verify an advocate’s credentials with the relevant State Bar Council before engaging their services.

4. Emergency Situations

In situations involving immediate physical danger, domestic violence, active theft, or medical emergencies, please do not wait for online categorization. Immediately dial the National Emergency Police Response Support System at 112, the Women Helpline at 1091, or the Cyber Crime Helpline at 1930.
